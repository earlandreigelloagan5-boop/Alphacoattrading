How the coats work together

On steel, each coat has its own job. This is the usual order of work.

  1. Prepare the steel

    Paint only holds on a clean surface. Remove rust, mill scale, oil and dust, and let the steel dry before the first coat.

  2. Prime with zinc-rich epoxy

    Alphacoat 3130 Epoxy Zinc Rich Primer goes on first. The zinc in the film protects the steel from rust and gives the next coat something to grip.

  3. Finish with a topcoat

    The topcoat gives the color and gloss, and shields the primer from sun, rain and wear.

For thinning, use the reducer that matches the paint: Thinner #40 for polyurethane coatings, Thinner #10 for lacquers. The right system depends on the surface and where it will be used, so tell us about the job and we will recommend one.
